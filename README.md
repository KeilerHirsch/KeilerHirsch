## About

I build practical software, automation and technical research systems. My work spans **software and systems engineering, security research, digital forensics, OSINT and AI-assisted development**, with a strong focus on reproducible evidence and engineering that survives contact with reality.

My foundation is hands-on engineering: precision mechanics, industrial electronics, applied systems work and independent technical experimentation. I look for **failure modes, tolerances, root causes, clear boundaries and measurable results** rather than impressive terminology.

**KeilerHirsch** is my developer identity; **[KeilerHirsch Labs](https://github.com/KeilerHirschLabs)** is the home for products and research. See GitHub's pinned repositories and the [live KHL repository list](https://github.com/orgs/KeilerHirschLabs/repositories) for current projects. Each project's README, releases and license are the source of truth — no manually maintained product catalogue here.

## How I build

- **Evidence before claims.** Reproducible tests, provenance and observable outcomes matter more than impressive status labels.
- **KISS at the surface.** The user experience should stay simple; stronger assurance belongs at boundaries where it pays off.
- **Determinism where it matters.** Prefer explicit state, rules and verifiable sources over unexplained decisions.
- **Engineering over theatre.** Use the language, architecture and checks the actual problem warrants.
- **Autonomy with verification.** Automate the repetitive parts, preserve safeguards, and check the real result.
