## Boundaries

Automated commits by `github-actions[bot]` are outputs of maintainer-authored workflow/software and are not treated as a separate human author.

Third-party pull-request titles, repository names, public GitHub facts, badges, service marks, linked content, and other externally sourced material remain subject to their actual source and applicable rights.

Generative-AI systems, editors, IDEs, linters, and coding assistants are treated as development tools, not authors or rights holders.
